# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering hiring managers and technical leaders evaluating Rudolf Rissling's engineering depth, production ownership, and ability to deliver complex systems.

## Product Purpose

Present a credible, evidence-led portfolio of Rudolf's professional work, founder-led products, technical capabilities, and career progression. Success means a hiring manager can quickly understand the scale, complexity, and production impact of his work and has a clear path to review his resume or make contact.

## Brand Personality

Technical, ambitious, and playful. The site should feel like an engineer's personal environment while keeping claims concrete and professional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate recruiting pages, anonymous SaaS marketing, inflated claims without evidence, and visual treatments that obscure technical substance. Preserve the established terminal, particle, dark-surface, and purple-accent aesthetic rather than introducing a competing visual language.

## Design Principles

- Lead with production impact and engineering scope.
- Make flagship work easy for hiring managers to identify and understand.
- Separate public work from confidential work without weakening the technical story.
- Keep technical personality, but never at the expense of readability or credibility.
- Prefer concise evidence over broad self-description.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain responsive layouts, semantic navigation, keyboard operability, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion behavior where animation is nonessential.
